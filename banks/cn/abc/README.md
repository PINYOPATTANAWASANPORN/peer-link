# Agricultural Bank of China (中国农业银行) Adapter

## Scope
- Domestic CNY outgoing personal transfers via Agricultural Bank of China personal online banking (个人网上银行) or mobile client.
- Supported currency: `CNY` (2 decimal places mapped to minor unit cents).

## Unsupported
- Scheduled or pending processing transfers
- Foreign currency remittances
- Batch corporate payroll or merchant card settlements
- Incoming credit transfers without sender record
